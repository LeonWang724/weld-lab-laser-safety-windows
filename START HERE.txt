WELD LAB LASER SAFETY

ON YOUR MAC
Double-click Weld Lab Laser Safety.app to immediately preview the SAFE display.
Open Settings to change sample reports or connect to the Windows display PC.
The Preview label marks local samples, separate from live lab reports.

ON THE WINDOWS DISPLAY PC
Copy Install Weld Lab Laser Safety.cmd onto the Windows PC and double-click it.
Setup downloads dependencies, creates a Desktop shortcut and opens the display.
First setup needs internet; Windows 7 requires SP1 and runtime/security updates.
After setup, use Weld Lab Laser Safety on the Desktop. Keep its command window open.
No microSD card or Arduino is required.

Staff controls: http://DISPLAY-PC:8765/?control=1
Replace DISPLAY-PC with the Windows computer name or IPv4 address.
These buttons report status; they do not operate physical lasers.
